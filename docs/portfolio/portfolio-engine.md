# Portfolio Engine

## Overview

The Portfolio Engine is responsible for analyzing, monitoring, and improving portfolio quality.

Its objective is not to maximize returns at all costs.

Its objective is to optimize:

- diversification
- risk-adjusted returns
- allocation efficiency
- long-term consistency

---

## Supported Asset Classes

The engine is designed to support:

- equities
- mutual funds
- ETFs
- IPO investments
- fixed deposits
- bonds
- gold
- silver
- crypto assets

---

## Portfolio Components

### Holdings

Tracks:

- quantity

- unrealized gains
- realized gains

### Watchlists

Tracks:

- opportunities

### Cash Allocation

Tracks:

- deployable capital
- emergency reserves
- investment reserves
