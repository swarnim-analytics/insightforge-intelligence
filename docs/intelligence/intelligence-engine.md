# Intelligence Engine

## Overview

The Intelligence Engine is the reasoning layer of InsightForge-Intelligence.

Its purpose is not to predict the future with certainty.

Its purpose is to:

- identify probabilities
- identify opportunities
- identify risks
- detect anomalies
- generate insights
- summarize complex information
- support decision making

The engine combines:

- quantitative analytics
- statistical analysis
- AI reasoning
- infrastructure monitoring
- portfolio intelligence

## Reasoning Framework

The engine follows:

```text
Data
  ↓
Analytics
  ↓
Scoring
  ↓
AI Interpretation
  ↓
Actionable Intelligence
```

The AI layer never replaces analytics.

The AI layer explains analytics.

---

## Confidence Framework

Every insight should include a confidence score.

Example:

```text
Confidence Scale

90-100  Very High
75-89   High
60-74   Moderate
40-59   Low
0-39    Very Low
```

Confidence is influenced by:

- data quality
- signal strength
- market conditions
- historical consistency

---

## Risk Framework

Every opportunity should include a risk classification.

```text
Low Risk
Moderate Risk
High Risk
Speculative
```

Risk assessment should consider:

- volatility
- liquidity
- concentration
- valuation
- macroeconomic conditions

---
